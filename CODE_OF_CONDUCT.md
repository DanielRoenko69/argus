# Code of Conduct

Argus follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and focus on
what is best for the project and its users. Harassment, discrimination,
personal attacks and publishing others' private information are not
tolerated in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers at
<hello.argus@proton.me>. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation to
enforce this code.
